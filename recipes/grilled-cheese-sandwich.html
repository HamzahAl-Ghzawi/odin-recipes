Grilled cheese sandwich

Home

Description

An extremely mouth watering golden-brown, buttery-toasted sandwich with gooey melted cheese pulling apart at the center.

Ingredients

  • 1 to 2 tablespoons softened butter
  • 2 slices white or sourdough bread
  • 2 slices cheddar or American cheese

Steps

  1. Prep the Bread: Spread butter evenly on one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Heat the Pan: Preheat a non-stick skillet over low to medium-low heat.
  3. Assemble: Place one slice of bread buttered-side-down in the pan. Add the cheese slices, then top with the second slice of bread, buttered-side-up.
  4. Toast and Flip: Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the bottom is golden brown. Flip carefully and cook for another 3 to 4 minutes until the second side is toasted and the cheese is completely melted.
  5. Serve: Remove from the pan, let it rest for a minute, slice diagonally, and enjoy!
