Lasagna

Home

Description

An Italian comfort food classic, this rich and cheesy lasagna features layers of savory meat sauce, tender pasta sheets, and a creamy mixture of ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan. Baked until golden brown and bubbling at the edges, it delivers a deeply satisfying blend of herbs, melted cheese, and hearty sauce in every bite—perfect for feeding a crowd or saving for incredible leftovers.

Ingredients

For the meat sauce:

  • 1 lb (450g) lean ground beef (100% Halal certified)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jar (24 oz / 680g) marinara sauce or passata
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

For the Cheese & Pasta Layers:

  • 12 lasagna sheets (boiled or oven-ready)
  • 15 oz (425g) ricotta cheese
  • 2 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese (microbial/vegetable rennet)
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

For the Side Salad (as pictured):

  • 2 cups fresh baby spinach or mixed greens
  • 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced into rings
  • Olive oil and lemon juice (or balsamic vinegar) for light dressing

Steps

  1. Prep the meat sauce: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and minced garlic, sautéing for about 2 to 3 minutes until softened and fragrant. Add the ground beef, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, and cook until browned throughout. Drain off any excess grease. Stir in the tomato paste, marinara sauce, dried oregano, dried basil, salt, and black pepper. Lower the heat and let it simmer uncovered for 10 to 15 minutes to let the flavors meld together.
  2. Mix the Cheese Filling: While the sauce simmers, combine the ricotta cheese, beaten egg, half of the grated Parmesan, and chopped fresh parsley in a mixing bowl. Season with a light pinch of salt and black pepper, then mix well until smooth and creamy. Set aside.
  3. Cook the Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Cook the lasagna sheets according to the package directions until just al dente (usually about 8–10 minutes). Drain the noodles and lay them flat on parchment paper or foil so they don't stick together.
  4. Assemble the Layers: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
    • Base: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce evenly across the bottom of the dish to keep the bottom layer of pasta from sticking.
    • First Layer: Lay 3 to 4 lasagna sheets side-by-side to cover the sauce. Spread one-third of the ricotta mixture over the noodles, followed by a layer of meat sauce and a generous sprinkle of shredded mozzarella.
    • Repeat: Add a second layer of noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat for a third layer.
    • Top: Finish with the remaining noodles, the last of the meat sauce, and top with the rest of the mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  5. Bake & Rest: Cover the baking dish loosely with aluminum foil (tent it slightly so it doesn't touch the top cheese layer) and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 to 20 minutes until the cheese is completely melted, golden brown, and bubbling at the edges.

Remove from the oven and let it rest for 15 minutes before slicing. This lets the layers set so it holds its shape cleanly when served!
